Questions

Frequently asked

How long does a school management system take to build?

It depends on how many modules you need. A focused system (records, marks and report cards) is typically ready in a few weeks, while a full suite with fees, portal access and reporting takes longer. You get an exact timeline in your written proposal.

Does the system need internet to work?

Not necessarily. We can install it to run on your own computers or local network so it works fully offline, or host it online so students and parents can log in from anywhere. Many clients use a combination of both.

Can you move our existing records into the new system?

Yes. If your data is in Excel, Access, another system or even structured paper registers, we can import it so you start with your history intact rather than from an empty database.

Will you train our staff?

Always. Training is part of every delivery. We work with the people who will use the system daily — teachers, bursars, clerks or cashiers — until they are comfortable, and we leave a simple user guide behind.

What happens if something breaks later?

You call or WhatsApp us. Every project includes a support period after launch, and we offer ongoing maintenance agreements for updates, fixes and new features as your needs change.

Do you work outside Kitwe?

Yes. Our offices are in Kitwe, but we serve clients across the Copperbelt and the rest of Zambia. Much of the work can be done remotely, with site visits arranged for installation and training.
